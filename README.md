## Kalimath Astrology

Astrologer and puja service in Panditwari, Dehradun. Started about 15 years ago by the late L.D. Bhatt and now run by the Bhatt brothers. Consultations in Hindi and English, in person or on a WhatsApp call.

- Website: [kalimathastrology.com](https://kalimathastrology.com)
- Free guna milan calculator: [kalimathastrology.com/guna-milan](https://kalimathastrology.com/guna-milan/)

### Open source

**[kalimath-vedic-astrology-tools](https://github.com/kalimathastrology-arch/kalimath-vedic-astrology-tools)**: a small JavaScript library for guna milan (Ashtakoota) scoring and nakshatra / Moon-sign lookup, with a [browser demo](https://kalimathastrology-arch.github.io/kalimath-vedic-astrology-tools/). It is the code behind the free calculator on our website.

Kalimath Astrology is a private service and is not affiliated with the Kalimath temple or its trust.
